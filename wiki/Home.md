# PSX-DS

PSX-DS is a PlayStation emulator for dual-screen handhelds, made first for the AYN Thor. The game runs on one screen; the other shows the game's own map or menu, kept up to date as you play, the way the DS Castlevanias work. It's filled from a hidden second copy of the game, so your game is never paused or touched.

PSX-DS is early work in progress: the emulator runs, and games get their second screens one at a time (see [[Supported games]]).

It's a fork of [GBA DS](https://github.com/kyannon/gba-ds) by kyannon, which does the same for Game Boy Advance games, and uses [SwanStation](https://github.com/libretro/swanstation) for PlayStation emulation.
